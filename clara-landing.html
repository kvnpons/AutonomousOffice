Questions practices usually ask

Do I need to switch practice management software?

No. Clara connects to what you already use and works alongside it — there's nothing to migrate.

What does my team still handle?

Anything Clara flags as unclear — an unusual denial, a mismatched amount, a new payer contract. Everything else posts on its own.

How is patient data protected?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and every action is written to an audit log your team can review at any time.

Most practices are fully enrolled and reconciling their first payments within two weeks.
